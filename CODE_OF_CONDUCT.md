# Code of Conduct

VIO is a community for people who enjoy music, careful engineering, and constructive learning. It is maintained independently by **Anuppaul**.

## Our expectations

We welcome contributors regardless of background, experience level, identity, musical tradition, accessibility needs, or location.

Please:

- Respect other contributors and musical practices.
- Offer actionable, evidence-based feedback; disagree with ideas without attacking people.
- Explain accessibility or device constraints when relevant.
- Keep public discussions free of harassment, threats, hate speech, sexualized remarks, personal information disclosure, spam, and intimidation.
- Give proper attribution and respect intellectual-property rights.
- Make space for beginners while welcoming rigorous technical discussion.

## Moderation

The maintainer may edit, hide, lock or remove abusive comments, close disruptive issues, request revisions, or restrict repeated offenders. Responses should be proportional to the conduct, impact and prior context.

For conduct complaints, contact the maintainer privately using an appropriate GitHub channel. Do not disclose personal details in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed in good faith, with confidentiality respected as far as practical.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and other VIO community spaces.
